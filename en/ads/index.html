Common questions

Are the ad accounts in my name?

Yes. We prefer your ad accounts and pages to stay in your name, with management access granted to us, so your data and accounts always remain yours.

Which platform should I start with?

It depends on what you sell and who your customer is. Some services are searched for on Google; some products sell through video on TikTok and Instagram. We suggest the right starting point once we understand your business.

When will I see results?

The first two weeks are usually for testing and learning; then we focus the budget on what works. We share the numbers from week one.

What if I already have campaigns running?

We review them first, keep what's worth keeping and fix the rest, changing things gradually instead of switching everything off at once.

Have a question about your campaign?

Tell us what you sell and where you advertise now, and we'll reply with a clear first step.
