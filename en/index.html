Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost?

There are no fixed prices. It depends on the service you need and the size of the work. Tell us what you need on WhatsApp and we'll send a clear offer before you start.

Will the ad accounts be in my name?

Yes. Ad accounts, pages and the domain stay in your name and you give us management access only, so your data always stays yours.

When will I see results?

The first two weeks are usually for testing and learning, then we focus the budget on what works. We share the numbers from week one.

I already have campaigns or a website. Do you start from scratch?

No. We review what you have first, keep what works and fix what needs fixing, gradually so your business never stops.

Do you work with businesses outside Jordan?

Yes. We work with businesses in Jordan and the Gulf and with Arab business owners in the US. Meetings are online.

Have a question about your campaign?

Tell us what you sell and what the problem is, and we'll reply with a clear first step.
