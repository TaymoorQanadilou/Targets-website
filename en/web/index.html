Common questions

Can the store take local online payments?

Yes. We connect local gateways such as Lahza and eSadad, international ones such as Stripe and PayPal, plus cash on delivery and delivery companies.

I already have a website. Can you improve it?

Yes. We review your current site, recommend what to fix and what to rebuild, and improve it step by step so your business never stops.

How long does a website take?

It depends on the size. A landing page is different from a full store or a custom system. Once we understand your project, we give you a clear timeline before we start.

Will the site and domain be in my name?

Yes. The domain and accounts stay yours, and we hand over full admin access.
